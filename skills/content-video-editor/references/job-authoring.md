# Job authoring guide

## Scene selection

| Kind | Best for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `hook` | first-second contrast, question, surprising framing | a plain title card |
| `cards` | three parallel routes, categories, comparisons | more than five cards |
| `process` | actions that happen in sequence | decorative timelines with no meaning |
| `network` | relationships, combinations, flows between roles | disconnected keywords |
| `summary` | a final choice, principle or compact recap | a second introduction |

## Presenter modes

- `full`: retain human connection while placing content in a protected side zone.
- `circle`: use for identity, commentary and continuity between animated sections.
- `none`: use when the animated explanation needs the whole frame.

The circular crop must be adjusted with job-level crop settings. Never assume one crop works for every source video.

## Copy rules

- Headline: preferably 4–14 Chinese characters or 3–9 English words.
- Subtitle: one complete thought, normally under 28 Chinese characters.
- Point label: preferably under 10 Chinese characters.
- Do not convert the transcript into on-screen paragraphs.
- Use numbers only when they come from the approved source or are clearly structural labels.

## Timing rules

- Use the source video's 30fps timeline by default.
- `sourceStartFrame` controls which source-video frame begins inside a scene.
- Scene durations must cover the entire approved video without accidental gaps.
- The full duration is the sum of every scene's `durationInFrames`.
